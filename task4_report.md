# Executive Decision Report & Business Insights

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes the recent e-commerce transaction data to understand purchasing patterns, optimize inventory, and improve total revenue performance across channels.

## Key Findings & KPIs
1. **Top Revenue Generator:** Laptops are our highest-grossing product category, contributing to over 80% of the calculated lifecycle revenue.
2. **Average Order Value (AOV):** Our team identified that the average cart value stands at approximately $531 per order.
3. **Data Integrity Issue Fixed:** Addressed critical bugs in data ingestion where negative quantity values and empty fields were skewing original sales reports.

## Strategic Business Decisions
* **Inventory Focus:** Restock High-Margin products like Laptops and Monitors before peak weekend hours.
* **Cross-Selling Opportunities:** Bundle lower-cost accessories like Mice and Keyboards with primary laptop purchases to maximize cart value.
